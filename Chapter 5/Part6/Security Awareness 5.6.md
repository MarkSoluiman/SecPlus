## Phishing Campaigns

By sending a crafted phishing emails to the organization's employees, the organization can know how many people clicked on the phishing email. 

This is an automated process. Centralized reporting for incorrect clicks. Users can receive immediate feedback and security training if they clicked the phishing email link. 

The aim is to train employees on how to spot a phishing email. They should look for spelling and grammatical errors, domain name and email inconsistencies, unusual attachments, request for personal information, etc.

## Anomalous Behavior Recognition

Organizations should be on the look for unusual activities on a user's workstation. Risky behaviors may include modifying hosts file, replacing a core OS file, uploading sensitive files, etc.

Unexpected behavior may include logging in from another country, increase in data transfers, etc.

Unintentional behavior may include typing the wrong domain name, misplacing USB drives, misconstruing security settings, etc. 

## Development 

Organizations should have a security awareness team and to determine roles for training, monitoring, policy creation, etc.

The team should establish a minimum awareness level by creating emails, posters, notices, training as a form of information delivery about security awareness.

The team can also create customized training to integrate  compliance mandates like PCI DSS, HIPAA, GDPR, etc.

The team should assess the performance of the security awareness programs and to make updates in lower performing areas
