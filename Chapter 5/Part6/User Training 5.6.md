## User Guidance and Training

Organizations should document all security requirements. Provide access online to policy guidelines. Reference the policies in the employee handbook.

Employees should have situational awareness. They should be always looking for threats like suspicious email links, attachments, unusual URLs, text messages, etc. 

Employees can also act as an insider threat. It is important to add multiple approvals for critical processes, monitor files and systems as much as possible.

User guidance and training should also include removable media and cables as those can contain malware or viruses.

Users need to recognize sensitive data and how to deal with this data to keep them secure and private.

Users who work from home bring more security concerns. Users need to always be connected by VPN when working remotely.
