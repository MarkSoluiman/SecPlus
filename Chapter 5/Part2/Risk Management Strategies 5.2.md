There are number of different strategies to deal with risk. One of these strategies is **transfer**.

In transfer strategy, organizations move the risk to another party or buying some cybersecurity insurance. 

**Accept** strategy is the organization simply saying that it will accept the risk.

Another variant of the Accept strategy is **accept with exemption**. For example,  A company has a legacy web server that is no longer supported by the vendor. Upgrading or replacing it immediately would break a critical business application. The security team decides to **accept the risk with exception**. They document the risk, restrict access to the server, monitor it closely, and plan to replace it within the next 12 months.

Another strategy is **avoid**. This is when organizations stop participating in a high risk activity which effectively removes the risk. A similar variant of avoid is mitigate which is to decrease the risk level. 

## Risk Reporting

This is a formal document to identify risks with detailed information for each risk. 

It is usually created for senior management to help them making decisions regarding resources, budgeting, additional security tasks, etc.
