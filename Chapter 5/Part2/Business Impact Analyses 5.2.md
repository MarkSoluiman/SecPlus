How long can business get up and running after an outage. This is called **Recovery Time Objective (RTO)**

**Recovery Point Objective** describes the least amount of data acceptable for the business to be running. For example, an organization would consider the last twelve months of data enough for operation. 

Measuring the time it takes to fix an issue is called **Mean Time to Repair (MTTR)**. This is an important metric for determining the cost and time associated with unplanned outages.

Measuring the estimated time a system will run between two outages is called **Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)**. It is measured by total uptime/ number of breakdowns. It can be used to plan and predict possible outages.
