## Risk Identification

Risk management helps to understand potential risks and to identify weaknesses before they become an issue.  As growth brings risk, It is useful to get ahead of any potential problems. 

## Performing a Risk Assessment 

Risk evaluation may only happen one time. For example, when the assessment be part of a one-time project or when a company is acquiring another organization or buying and installing new equipment. 

Risk assessment might also be an ongoing process. For example, it may be part of an existing process, or during a change control.

## Ad Hoc Assessments 

Ad Hoc means for this. So when an organization is performing an ad hoc risk assessment, it is performing it for one specific purpose. For example, a new type of cyber attack started to happen in the wild. The organization wants to make sure its systems and infrastructure is not prone to this new attack.

In a case of Ad hoc risk assessment, a committee is created and the risk assessment proceeds. Once the assessment is complete, the committee is disbanded. 

## Recurring Assessments 

The evaluation occurs on standard intervals. For example, if the organization is keeping credit cards info, the **Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)** requires annual risk assessments.
