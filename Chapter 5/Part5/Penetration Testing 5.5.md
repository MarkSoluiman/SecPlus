## Working Knowledge

There are three types of environment that pen-testers work on. Known environment which means that the pen-tester knows everything about the targeted environment. Partially known environment, which is a mix of known and unknown. Often used when an organization wants the pen-tester to focus on certain systems within the environment. Unknown environment, which means the pen-tester doesn't know anything about the targeted environment (Blind test).

## Reconnaissance

This is the information gathering step before performing the pen-testing.

Reconnaissance has two types, passive and active.

## Passive Reconnaissance 

A pen-tester will gather info about the targeted environment from open sources and by not interacting directly with the environment. 

## Active Reconnaissance

A pen-tester will gather info about the targeted environment by directly interacting with the systems within the environment. For example, port scanning using Nmap tool is active reconnaissance.
