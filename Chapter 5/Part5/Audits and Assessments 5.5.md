Cyber security audit examines the IT infrastructure, software, devices, etc. It checks for effectiveness of policies and procedures. Find vulnerabilities before the attackers. Can be performed internally or by a third-party.

**Attestation** is an opinion of truth or accuracy of an company's security position that is associated with the results of an audit.

## Internal Audits

The audit committee oversees risk management activities. All audits start and stop with the committee. 

Internal audits often start with self-assessments. This allows the organization to look at their internal processes and procedures and see how well they match the requirements of the organization.

## External Audits

Some organizations or compliance regulations require an external audit done by a third-party. Audit type and frequency are often based on the regulation. 

External audits require hands-on research, view records, compile reports, etc.
