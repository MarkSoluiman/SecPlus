## Change Management 

A formal process for managing change to avoid downtime, confusion, and mistakes.

Determine the scope of the change, analyze the risk associated with the change, create a plan, get end-user approval, present the proposal to the change control board, have a backup plan if the change doesn't work, document the change.

## Onboarding 

Create accounts. Associate the user with the proper groups and departments.  Provide required IT hardware like laptops, phones, etc.

## Offboarding

The procedures should be pre-planned and answers what happens to the hardware and the data saved on that hardware. Account information  should be deactivated but not always deleted. 

## Playbooks

A step-by-step set of processes and procedures. Used as a manual checklist or to create automated activities. 

This is often integrated with **Security Orchestration, Automation and Response (SOAR)** platform. 

## Governance Structures 

Boards are a panel of specialists who set the tasks or requirements for the committees.

Committees are subject-matter experts who consider the input from a board. Determines the next steps for a topic at hand. Presents the results to the board.

Different concerns are associated with government agencies such as legal concerns, administrative requirements, political issues, etc.

There are two different ways to approach governance, centralized and decentralized. 

Centralized governance is located in one location with a group of decision makers.

Decentralized governance spreads the decision-making process around to other individuals or locations.
