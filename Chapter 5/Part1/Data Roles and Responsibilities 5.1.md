Data owner is accountable for specific data, often a senior officer. For example, a vice president of sales owns the customer relationship data and a treasurer would own the financial information.

Data controller manages the purposes and means which personal data is processed.

Data processor processes data on behalf of the data controller. Often a third-party or different group.

For example, a data controller like the payroll department defines payroll amounts and time frames. A data processor like the payroll company processes payroll and stores employee information.  

Data custodian/steward is responsible for data accuracy, privacy, and security. 

He/She works directly with the data. Associates sensitivity labels to the data, ensures compliance with any applicable laws and standards, manages and access rights to the data, implements security controls.
