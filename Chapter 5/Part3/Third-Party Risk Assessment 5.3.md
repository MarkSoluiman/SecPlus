## Right-to-Audit Clauses 

Companies often share the same data with other companies. This is true when companies relies on other companies for services like payroll. This shared data is risk for both companies, if one company got breached, any other company that is dealing with it will have the risk to get breached as well. This is why Right-to-audit should be in the contract which is a legal agreement to have the option to perform a security audit at any time.

## Evident of Internal Audits

To eliminate any biases for the company that is being audited against, it is common to have a third-party organization performing the audit. 

The security audit checks for access management, off boarding, password policies, VPN controls, etc. 

## Supply Chain Analysis

Organizations have to analyze the journey of a product that they produce or manage starting from the vendors to the customer. Evaluate coordination between groups involved, identify areas of improvement, document the business process changes, etc. 

## Vendor Monitoring

This is the ongoing management of the vendor relationship. The reviews should occur on a regular basis including financial health checks, IT security reviews, news articles, social media posts, etc. 

## Questionnaires 

A common way to perform vendor monitoring is to send out a questionnaire to the vendor. Question might include what is the vendor's due diligence process? what plans are in place for disaster recovery? what secure storage method is used for company data? ,etc.
