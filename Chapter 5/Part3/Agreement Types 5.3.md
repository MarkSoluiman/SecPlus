## Common Agreements

If an organization is dealing with a service provider, it may want to set a **Service Level Agreement (SLA)** with the service provider to set the minimum terms for services provided. This can be certain requirements for up time or availability. 

If an organization is entering into a partnership with a third party, it mat want to set a **Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)**. Both sides agree to common goals. It may include statements of confidentiality.

The next step of MOU is **Memorandum of Agreement (MOA)**. This is where both sides agree to the objectives. Unlike the MOU, MOA can be a legal document even without legal language. Unlike the contract, may not contain legally enforceable promises. 

For longer relationships between organizations, **Master Service Agreement (MSA)** is used. This is a legal contract and agreement of terms. Might have a broad framework to cover later transactions. Many detailed negotiations happen here. Future projects will be based on this agreement.

For more details in agreements, **Work Order (WO) / Statement of Work (SOW)** can be used. This is a specific list of items to be completed.  Often used in conjunction with MSA. Details the scope of the job location, deliverables schedule, acceptance criteria, etc.  

**Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**. This agreement protects the trade secrets, business activities of organizations working with each other. The NDA can be unilateral (one-way NDA) or bilateral/multilateral.

Business Partners Agreement (BPA). This agreement describes the financial details associated with the agreement between organizations and describes what type of ownership stake is acquired. BPA also defines how the business will work in partnerships. For example, who will make the business decisions.
