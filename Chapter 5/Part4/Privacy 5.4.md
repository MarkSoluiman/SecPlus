## General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

This is the European Union privacy regulation. It regulates the data protection and privacy for individuals in the EU. Information may include names, addresses, email addresses, bank details, etc. 

## Data Subject

The GDPR defines data subjects as the data that belongs to every individual in the EU. 

## Data Inventory and Retention

Some organizations have data inventory which is a listing of all managed data. The inventory includes the owner of the data, update frequency, format of the data, etc.
